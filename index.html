Assumptions: exact catenary shape for a hanging wire, tension dropping at each block by the block efficiency entered (multi-span helicopter pulls), a Helistringer brake that holds a fixed torque so wire tension rises as the reel empties, a straight long line, and no wind, ice, rotor wash or aircraft motion. Wire in span is the wire hanging between attachments only; add wire for tie-offs, dead-ends, sagging and reserve on the reel. Load limits must come from the aircraft flight manual supplement for the equipment installed. Keep line and conductor tension within a 5:1 safety factor of the rated breaking strength.

References: IEEE Std 524-2016, Guide for the Installation of Overhead Transmission Line Conductors (Annex F block efficiency, reel braking tension, helicopter pulls, grounding and crossing structures); OSHA 29 CFR 1926.1408 Table A (minimum approach distances); FAA AC 133-1B, Rotorcraft External-Load Operations.
